# ?? JAROOS v1.0.0 – Interactive Educational App for Kids
**Learn • Play • Grow**

Official major project v1.0.0 release of **JAROOS**, featuring an offline-first Flutter mobile client and Node.js REST API with MySQL backend.

---

### ?? Highlights & Features

#### ?? 1. Playful Mascot & Animated Game Splash Screen
- **Game-Style Animated Splash Screen**: Custom 3D cartoon splash artwork with bubbly  JAROOS title, golden Learn • Play • Grow ribbon banner, animal companions, rotating radiant sunburst beams, drifting fluffy clouds, and interactive touch magic sparkles.
- **Custom Mascot App Icon**: Handcrafted lime-green (#8CE600) monster mascot face with big expressive eyes, dual-sparkle highlights, and white hair tuft across all Android mipmap densities (mdpi, hdpi, xhdpi, xxhdpi, xxxhdpi).
- **Onboarding & Auth Flow**: Warm cream landing screen (WelcomeScreen) with tactile pill buttons, lime-green login and registration screens with peeking vector mascot animations.

#### ?? 2. Full Curriculum Across 10 Learning Modules
1. **Alphabet**: A to Z with phonics, interactive dialogs, and native TTS speech.
2. **Numbers**: 1 to 20 with interactive counting stars and audio narration.
3. **Colors**: Primary, secondary, and rainbow colors with real-world mixing tips.
4. **Shapes**: Geometry exploration (circles, squares, triangles, stars) with side counters.
5. **Animals**: Jungle, farm, ocean, and arctic animal encyclopedias with cheerful voiceovers.
6. **Fruits & Veggies**: Healthy nutrition exploration and category filtering.
7. **Bedtime Stories**: Moral stories with full read-to-me audio reader.
8. **Fun Rhymes**: Classic musical rhymes and lyrics.
9. **Quiz Arena**: Multiple-choice trivia with star rewards, instant visual/audio feedback, and score tracking.
10. **My Progress**: Interactive mastery telemetry, trophy room, and achievement badges.

#### ?? 3. AI Playground
- **Sparky AI Buddy**: Child-safe curiosity companion with safety moderation filter (COPPA principles).
- **Story Magic Generator**: Dynamic child-directed moral story engine awarding reward coins.

#### ??? 4. Parent Dashboard & Gate
- Screen time wellness controls (15m to 60m limits).
- PIN Gate (1234) and adult math challenges (8 × 4 = ?).
- Real-time learning telemetry and progress analytics.

#### ?? 5. Audio & Speech Engine
- Text-to-speech engine (lutter_tts) with Android 11+ package visibility queries enabled.
